PLAIN-ENGLISH GUIDE

Help & glossary

A few words you may see while setting up your experiment.

⌁

Magnetic field

A magnetic environment that helps guide charged particles. A larger value means a stronger field.

◌

Density

How many particles are present in a space. More density means more particles in the plasma.

ϟ

Temperature

Here, temperature means particle energy. It tells you how energetic electrons or ions are.

↔

What this tool does

It helps you explore a set of plasma conditions before a backend simulation produces a scientific result.
